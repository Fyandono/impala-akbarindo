## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored by your browser when you visit a website. They help the site remember your choices or understand how it is used.

## Cookies we use

| Name        | Category  | Purpose                                   | Duration  |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------- |
| `cc_cookie` | Necessary | Stores your cookie consent choices        | 12 months |
| `_ga`       | Analytics | Distinguishes visitors (Google Analytics) | 2 years   |
| `_ga_<ID>`  | Analytics | Stores session state (Google Analytics)   | 2 years   |

Analytics cookies are set **only** after you give consent. If you decline or withdraw consent, analytics cookies are removed.

## Managing your choices

You can change your choices at any time via the "Cookie Settings" button at the bottom of the page, or through your browser settings.
