## 1. Scope

This policy explains how [Company Name] ("we") processes data when you visit this site, with reference to Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection.

## 2. Data we process

This site has **no** forms, accounts, or login features, so we do not directly request personal data such as your name or email.

If you consent to analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to collect aggregated usage data, such as pages visited, approximate location (city/country level), device type, and browser.

## 3. Legal basis

Analytics data is processed only on the basis of your **consent**. You can withdraw consent at any time via the "Cookie Settings" link at the bottom of every page.

## 4. Storage and third parties

Analytics data is processed by Google LLC as a data processor. We disable advertising features and Google Signals. Data is retained according to the retention period we configure in Google Analytics.

## 5. Your rights

Under Indonesian law, you have the right to obtain information about, access, correct, and delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent to its processing.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address listed on the Contact page.
